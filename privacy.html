Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026

10 to Central is a number puzzle game for Android. This page explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: the game itself doesn't collect anything about you. Ads and purchases are handled by Google, and Google's own policies apply to those.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (Daily results and streaks, Line journey records, Endless totals), your settings, and whether you've bought Remove ads are saved only on your device. There is no account, and none of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a full-screen ad between some puzzles, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's AdMob software may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version and language
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information such as crash and performance data

Google may use this to show personalised ads. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Android settings (Settings → Security & privacy → Ads on most phones).

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

Remove ads is a one-off purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the game only receives confirmation that you own Remove ads, never your card or payment details. The Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms apply to purchases.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't run our own servers, analytics or tracking.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
