10 to Central

Make 10 from the four digits of a train carriage number.

Add, subtract, multiply and divide the four numbers on the carriage to make exactly 10. Some carriages can't be done, so call Impossible when you're sure. A new Daily carriage every morning, a station-by-station journey along eight lines, and an endless commute.
